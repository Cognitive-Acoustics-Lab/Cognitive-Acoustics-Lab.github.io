01

Auditory cognition in three-dimensional space

We extend the knowledge of auditory cognition, built mainly on the frontal horizontal plane, to three-dimensional space including the rear, above and below, and depth, and examine how distance perception, attention, emotion, and peripersonal space differ by direction.

  • attention
  • depth perception
  • peripersonal space
  • anisotropy
02

Multisensory design of auditory information

We study interactions among vision, audition, and the body from the viewpoints of cognitive characteristics such as attention and of impression and affect, and apply the findings to in-vehicle warning sounds, VR environments, and interactive art.

  • multimodal
  • crossmodal
  • auditory signs
  • VR
03

Perception and modulation of body-generated sounds

We examine how sounds generated by bodily actions, such as footsteps and the rustle of clothing, affect animacy, impressions, and body representation, and develop techniques that modulate impressions and bodily sensation by manipulating acoustic features.

  • embodiment of sound
  • footsteps
  • animacy
  • body representation
