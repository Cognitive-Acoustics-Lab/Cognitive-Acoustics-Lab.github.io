Research

We cover a broad range of topics in acoustics related to cognition.

01

Auditory cognition in three-dimensional space

Knowledge of auditory cognition has been built mainly on the frontal horizontal plane. We extend the scope to three-dimensional space, including the rear, above and below, and depth, and examine how auditory cognitive characteristics such as distance perception, attention, emotion, and peripersonal space differ by direction (anisotropy), aiming at a systematic account of auditory cognition in 3D space.

  • attention
  • depth perception
  • emotion
  • peripersonal space
  • anisotropy
02

Multisensory design of auditory information

We investigate multisensory interactions among vision, audition, and the body from two angles: cognitive characteristics such as attention, and impression and affect. The findings are applied to the design of auditory information, including in-vehicle warning sounds, VR environments, and interactive art.

  • multimodal
  • crossmodal
  • VR
  • auditory signs
  • human interface

03

Perception and modulation of body-generated sounds

Sounds generated by bodily actions, such as footsteps and the rustle of clothing, make us strongly aware of the presence of others and of the state of our own body. We examine how these sounds affect animacy, impressions, and body representation, and develop techniques that modulate impressions and bodily sensation by manipulating perceptually meaningful acoustic features.

  • embodiment of sound
  • footsteps
  • animacy
  • body representation
  • auditory feedback
  • timbre
